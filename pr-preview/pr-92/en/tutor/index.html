A personal tutor who can help you on your journey

Every page and every exercise of an explainer app has an address and exists as readable text. Your AI assistant can open it, read it and refer to it. So it knows what is explained, which exercise you solved and what you typed.

That lets it accompany, guide and support you: patient, personal and always there. This happens in your assistant, with your provider. lernapps.net learns nothing about it.

Try the self-assessment →

How it works

This is how it runs in Mathe-Karte, an explainer app.

  1. 1

    Assess yourself

    You do the self-assessment of an app. At the end it shows for each page: “You probably know this”, “Have another look” or “Read it closely”. The result stays on your device.

  2. 2

    Choose an assistant

    You choose your assistant: Claude, ChatGPT, Mistral Vibe, or “Copy text” for any other. You use your own account with the provider.

  3. 3

    Let it guide you

    One click gives the assistant your result and what you typed. It sends you page by page to what does not stick yet. Explaining and practising happen on the app's pages.

  4. 4

    Learn on the page

    At the top of each page you switch on “Learn with an AI tutor”. After a wrong answer, “Why?” gives the tutor the exercise, your input and the app's message. “Back to the tutor” tells it what you practised.

  5. 5

    Assess again

    When the pages of a chapter stick, you do a new round. After the result, the assistant decides how to go on.

How the tutor works

Your assistant gets these rules from the app. You can read them first.

  • It asks instead of solving

    The tutor names the idea behind the mistake and asks a guiding question. It does not give the answer.

  • It starts with the picture

    It explains with the picture on the page and asks first: what do you see?

  • Calculating happens on paper

    You type the result into the app. In the chat there are estimating and why questions.

  • Done is what you can explain

    A page counts as done when three exercises were right and you can explain one of them. Then it asks for an exercise by hand: a photo of the sheet only, without a name. It checks every step and names the first wrong one instead of correcting it.

  • The video comes last

    Exercises come first. It recommends the video when you cannot repeat the explanation after two tries.

  • After assessing, it keeps it short

    It answers with three sentences at most, one question and a link to the next page.

You see what your tutor gets

On every page and in the self-assessment there is the link “See what your tutor gets”. Parents and teachers can read the whole brief before a child passes it on.

Example: the brief for the self-assessment “Probability” →

  1. 1 Self-assessment in the app
  2. 2 One click copies your result
  3. 3 You paste it into your assistant
  4. 4 It sends you to the next page

No lernapps.net server in between.

Privacy

No AI in lernapps.net

There is no AI built into lernapps.net, and we run no chatbot. The apps only make their learning content readable for your assistant. Everything else happens in your assistant, with your provider, to whom you agreed separately there. lernapps.net learns nothing about it.

The app sends nothing. One click gives the assistant your result and what you typed, and you can read the brief first. This is voluntary. Everything works without AI too.

Honest about limits

  • ●

    An assistant can be wrong.

    The checked source is the app's pages. If something does not match, the page counts.

  • ●

    AI does not replace people.

    Learning is also a shared experience: with friends, parents, teachers. An assistant is patient and always there, but it is not in the room when you practise together.

  • ●

    We cannot check how your assistant behaves.

    The rules are a request to it. That is not a guarantee.

  • ●

    Everything works without AI.

    The pages, the exercises and the app's messages (“Not right. First count all possible outcomes …”) work without an assistant.
