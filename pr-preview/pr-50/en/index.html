Germany's schools have a digitalisation problem.

And it is not about Wi-Fi or iPads, but about solutions that fit.

📚 Teacher

„ The lesson starts in ten minutes. I am looking for something my class can use to try out fractions themselves. Everything I find wants a sign-up or runs on servers I don't know. Am I even allowed to use it? I'll take the worksheet again. “

🏠 Parent

„ Tomorrow my son has a maths test. He is sitting at the kitchen table and doesn't understand percentages. I am looking for an app he can practise with. All I find is ads, subscriptions and apps that want an account first. “

🔧 Built it myself

„ Over the weekend I built a small app my daughter can use to play with probability. She loves it. Would it help other children too? No idea. It sits on GitHub, and nobody finds it. “

🎒 Student

„ The test is the day after tomorrow, and I just don't get functions. Videos don't help me. I want to try it out myself. “
↓ Sounds familiar?

Good apps exist. They just don't arrive.

People build small learning apps for their class or their child. With the help of AI this often takes a weekend. But the apps don't reach other classes. Whoever wants to use one carries the risk alone: Am I allowed to? Is it safe? And whoever builds one never learns whether it helped anywhere else.

lernapps.net brings these sides together.

What lernapps.net does for you

A fitting app, in your lesson in a few minutes.

The lesson is about to start. You need an app for exactly this topic. On lernapps.net you search by topic and grade. The app runs right away in the browser: without installation and without sign-up.

After the lesson you can say thanks with one click, or briefly tell us how it went. We don't need more.

How lernapps.net works

lernapps.net is free. Nobody earns money from it. There are no ads, and we collect no data in secret. The platform lives on two things: people build good learning apps. And others say thanks for them.

  • Free and without ads

    The apps run in your browser. That needs no expensive servers. This is why lernapps.net costs nothing.

  • What you type in stays on your device

    We only list apps that need no server of their own. They need no installation and no sign-up.

  • We count openly, not in secret

    We only count clicks that someone makes on purpose, for example “Thanks”. We only store the total. We don't know who clicked.

  • Thanks keeps it all alive

    A thanks shows the people who build apps that their work is worth it. Without this thanks there would soon be no new apps.

If you look after data protection at your school

The apps only run in the browser. They have no server of their own, no accounts and no cookies.

On the platform we only count clicks that someone makes on purpose: “Thanks”, “Used in class”, “I'll take a look” and short feedback. We only store the total of these clicks. We store no IP addresses and nothing in the browser. No data of students is created.

Why is this free?

The apps run in your browser. We don't need expensive servers for that. Whoever builds apps does it anyway for their own class or their own child. That is why lernapps.net can be free, without ads and without selling data.

What the platform needs is your thanks. It shows the people who build apps that their work is worth it.

“This won't work anyway.”

Maybe. We don't know yet. That is why we start small: with a few apps and a few people who build and use them. We wrote down beforehand how we will notice whether it works. And how we will notice that it doesn't.

We planned lernapps.net with the Platform Design Toolkit, a method for designing platforms. It states who takes part, what everyone gets out of it and what we try first. Everything is public.
