Get involved

lernapps.net lives on people who build apps, and on people who use them and say thanks. Pick where you stand.

  • libraryIf you teach
  • houseIf you help a child learn
  • backpackIf you are learning yourself
  • wrenchIf you want to build an app
  • puzzleIf you want to work on the platform

If you teach

Use apps and tell us how it went.

Use an app in class or when you cover a lesson. Afterwards you can say thanks with one click or give short feedback on whether it fitted. Tell your colleagues about it. And if an app is missing: tell us, or build it yourself.

Ask your AI assistant

Copy this text into your AI assistant. It reads lernapps.net and explains what the platform does for you.

I teach and I am looking for digital learning material. Read https://lernapps.net/llms.txt and the pages it links to. Explain to me in plain words what lernapps.net is, what the platform offers me as a teacher and how it keeps things safe. Also have a look at the catalogue: https://lernapps.net/apps/en/. Be honest about the limits.

If you help a child learn

Practise together and say thanks.

Look for an app on the topic your child needs help with. Did it help? A thank-you shows the person who built it that their work is worth it. And share the link with other parents, for example in the class group.

If you are learning yourself

Try things out instead of just watching.

Find an app on your topic. You don't need an account, and nobody knows who you are. Is there none yet? Maybe you build one yourself, with your AI assistant.

Ask your AI assistant

Copy this text into your AI assistant. It reads lernapps.net and explains what the platform does for you.

I am learning something right now and I am looking for something to try out. Read https://lernapps.net/llms.txt and the pages it links to. Explain to me briefly what lernapps.net is, what it does for my learning and how it keeps things safe. Also have a look at the catalogue (https://lernapps.net/apps/en/): is there already something on my topic? If not: think with me about whether we can build a small app for it together.

If you want to build an app

Build the app that is still missing.

You bring the idea and the content, your AI assistant builds it with you. lernapps.net gives you foundations, tools and checks. Your app stays yours. Whoever uses it can say thanks, and you hear about it.

If you want to work on the platform

Make lernapps.net better.

The platform is open: docs, tooling, catalog and this site are on GitHub. You can report bugs, improve texts, help build the tooling or think along about where it goes next.
