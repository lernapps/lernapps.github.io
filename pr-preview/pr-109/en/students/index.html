How the apps help you

Find your topic, open the app, start.

  • You don't understand a topic yet

    Find your topic and grade. The app explains step by step. You can look at it as often as you like.

    Apps by topic →

  • A test is coming up

    Practise exactly the material that will come up, until it sticks. If no app fits, your AI assistant makes one from your worksheets, just for you.

    Practise for a test →

  • You need someone who explains patiently

    In every explainer app you can hand the task to your AI assistant. It gives you hints instead of answers, also in the evening. lernapps.net learns nothing about it.

    How the tutor works →

  • You want to practise without anyone watching

    No account, no grade. Nobody knows who you are. Mistakes are allowed; that is how you learn.

  • You want to try things out yourself

    Many apps let you do something: roll dice, move things, decide. So you see for yourself how it works.
