Use apps

Find and use apps

For the next lesson, without preparing the night before.

  • You take over a class at short notice, perhaps outside your subject

    Pick subject, topic and grade. The apps explain themselves what to do, even if it isn't your subject.

    • Into class by link, QR code or projector
    • Runs on tablet, laptop and phone
    Apps by subject and grade →

  • The next lesson lacks a fitting exercise

    Your AI assistant turns your material into a practice app for exactly your topic. It runs under lernapps.net for a while, without you publishing anything.

    Have an exercise made →

  • Your class should do something itself

    In the apps the children try things out, decide and practise. They see right away whether it is right, instead of only watching.

    Example: probability with the Kniffel trainer →

  • You are responsible for data protection

    Every app runs under lernapps.net, with one privacy notice for all. You can pass it on to your head teacher.

    • No account, no cookies, no tracking
    • Nothing goes to third parties without a click; what an app loads is checked
    Privacy notice for your school →

  • Some children need you right now

    The app accompanies the class while it practises, each child at their own pace and with feedback. So your hands are free for the children who need particular attention.

Build an app for others

Build an app for others

Your AI assistant builds, you bring the material and the experience. Other classes learn with it, and the app stays yours.

  • You want to make your material digital

    Choose the content that gains most from being digital. Step by step you build your own digital collection, which other classes can use too.

    • Self-checking: the children see for themselves whether it is right
    • Interactivity: try out, move, decide
    • AI tutor: accompanies the task, with hints instead of answers
    Build an app →
