“This won't work anyway.”

Maybe. We don't know yet. That is why we start small: with a few apps and a few people who build and use them. We wrote down beforehand how we will notice whether it works. And how we will notice that it doesn't.

We planned lernapps.net with the Platform Design Toolkit, a method for designing platforms. It states who takes part, what everyone gets out of it and what we try first. Everything is public.
