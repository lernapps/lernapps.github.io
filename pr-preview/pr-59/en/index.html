🔒For us, privacy is a feature

No account, no cookies, no tracking. What someone types stays on their device. What lernapps.net stores and what it doesn't is set out in the privacy notice. You can pass it on to your head teacher or data protection officer. To the privacy notice →

That is all you need to know. The rest of this page is for everyone who wants to build an app themselves. To the apps →

A solid foundation for makers.

lernapps are not products of a publisher. Parents and teachers build them because their child or their class was missing something. lernapps.net gives them a foundation on which these become good apps.

The makers bring

  • ●their idea
  • ●their knowledge of the subject
  • ●their experience from the classroom and the kitchen table

The platform brings

  • ●foundations for a quiz, an explainer and an interactive app
  • ●a method: based on the curriculum, exercises with feedback
  • ●tools to build, check and list an app

That makes it quick and easy to turn your own ideas into apps.

What the apps can do →

„Another platform? There are plenty already.“

True. But this one belongs to no company.

lernapps.net is not the product of a vendor or an agency. It is a shared place: parents and teachers build apps here, share them and use them. The same principles apply to everyone.

  1. 1

    Nobody earns money from it.

    No subscription, no ads, no selling of data. Not later either. The apps need no server of their own, so running them costs almost nothing. More about this →

  2. 2

    A place to share.

    Some build apps, others use them. Feedback and thanks reach the people who built them.

  3. 3

    Open, with clear principles.

    No account, the apps collect nothing. The rules, the plan and the source code of the platform are public.
