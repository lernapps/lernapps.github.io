## Exercises

### gemische-finden (multiple-choice)

- Link: [./#gemische-finden](./#gemische-finden), in the section „Practice“
- Task: Which of these substances are mixtures?
- Answer format: choose all right answers
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ air – Air consists of nitrogen, oxygen and other gases. Several substances are mixed.
  - ✓ milk – Milk contains water, fats and proteins, among other things. It is a mixture.
  - ✗ pure water – Pure (distilled) water consists only of water. It is a pure substance.
  - ✗ pure oxygen – Pure oxygen consists of only one kind of substance. It is a pure substance.
- Solution: air; milk
- Explanation: Mixtures consist of several substances. Air and milk are mixtures; pure water and pure oxygen are pure substances.

### salzwasser-reinstoff (true-false)

- Link: [./#salzwasser-reinstoff](./#salzwasser-reinstoff), in the section „Practice“
- Task: Salt water is a pure substance.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is false.
- Explanation: Salt water consists of water and salt. Two substances are mixed, so it is a mixture. If the water evaporates, the salt stays behind.
