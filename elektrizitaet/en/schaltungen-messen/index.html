Circuits with meters

Predict and calculate current and voltage in series and parallel circuits, switch on and check on the meter.

  1. Series circuit with meters In a series circuit the current is the same everywhere, and the voltages of the resistors add up.
  2. Parallel circuit with meters In a parallel circuit every branch has the same voltage, and the branch currents add up to the total current.
