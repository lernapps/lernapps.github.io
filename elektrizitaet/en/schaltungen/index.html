Circuits

Switches, circuit symbols, series and parallel circuits. On the pages you build circuits yourself and see how the current runs.

  1. Switches and circuit symbols A switch opens and closes the circuit. In a circuit diagram you draw parts as circuit symbols.
  2. Series circuit In a series circuit the lamps are connected one after the other. If one fails, all go out.
  3. Parallel circuit In a parallel circuit every lamp has its own path. If one fails, the others keep shining.
