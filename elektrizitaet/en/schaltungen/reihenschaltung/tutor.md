## Exercises

### rs-ausfall (true-false)

- Link: [./#rs-ausfall](./#rs-ausfall), in the section „Practice“
- Task: In a series circuit of two lamps, the second keeps shining when the first is unscrewed.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is false.
- Explanation: In a series circuit there is only one path for the current. If a lamp is missing, the circuit is open and the other goes out too.

### rs-wege (single-choice)

- Link: [./#rs-wege](./#rs-wege), in the section „Practice“
- Task: How many paths does the current have in a series circuit?
- Answer format: choose one answer
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ Exactly one. – The parts hang one behind the other, so the current has only one way around the circuit.
  - ✗ One for each lamp. – Separate paths for each lamp exist in a parallel circuit, not in a series circuit.
  - ✗ None; the lamps shine because of the battery. – Without a path for the current no lamp shines. The circuit must be closed.
- Solution: Exactly one.
- Explanation: In a series circuit there is only one path, on which the current flows through all parts one after the other.

### rs-schalterplatz (true-false)

- Link: [./#rs-schalterplatz](./#rs-schalterplatz), in the section „Practice“
- Task: In a series circuit with a battery, a switch and two lamps it does not matter whether the switch sits before, between or after the lamps.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is true.
- Explanation: The current runs through all parts one after the other. It does not matter where the switch sits: when you open it, the circuit is broken and all lamps go out.

### schaltung-bauen (interactive exercise SchaltungBauen)

- Link: [./#schaltung-bauen](./#schaltung-bauen), in the section „Practice“
- What to do: You build a circuit from lamps, switches and wires that does what the task asks, switch the power on and test it.
- Answer format: The circuit built on the board (lamp, switch or wire per place); then “Check”.
- What counts as right: The circuit behaves as the task says: lamps shine, switches and unscrewed lamps act as asked. Any build that does this counts, not only the model solution; an additional switch is only a mistake where the task names a fixed number.
- Typical mistakes and the app's message:
  - The wrong number of lamps or switches.
  - One branch stays empty while the other holds a lamp: a short circuit, the lamps stay dark.
  - The parts are right, but a switch switches the wrong lamps or the lamps are in series instead of parallel.
- Tasks by number, 1 to 1: each a different one (the app shows “Task no. …”):
  - [Task no. 1](./?schaltung-bauen=1#schaltung-bauen): Build a circuit with two lamps one behind the other, that is, in series. When you unscrew one lamp, the other should go out too. The circuit board is a circuit with two places. You choose lamp, switch or wire, tap a place and then switch the power on. → Solution: A lamp on each of the two places, a switch is allowed. If you unscrew one, the other goes out.
